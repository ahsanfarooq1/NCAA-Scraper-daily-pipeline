READING THE NUMBERS
-------------------
There are TWO different "0-stat" ideas, and they are not the same thing:

1) 0-STAT GAME (Table 3): a game with NO player boxscore on either side.
   These were the "scraped too early" empty shells. The re-scrape fixed almost
   all of them: MBB D1 886 -> 0, D2 534 -> 23, D3 321 -> 24. The women's runs
   ended at D1 5, D2 17, D3 50. The ~119 still-empty games are genuinely empty
   at the NCAA (no boxscore was ever posted) and cannot be recovered.

2) 0-STAT TEAM (Table 1): a team whose SEASON totals are all zero. This count
   barely moved (about 106 before and after) because these are NOT broken data
   - they are NON-DIVISION opponents (e.g. Huston-Tillotson, Evangel, Biblical
   Stud. (TX), New College (FL)) that a D1/D2/D3 team played. The NCAA only
   tracks player stats for its own division, so the opponent shows a team row
   with empty players even in a fully-populated game. They have low GP (1-9) and
   are expected; the empty-GAME recovery does not touch them.

WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGED FROM FEBRUARY TO NOW
------------------------------------------
* Games: MBB grew by ~395 / 253 / 298 (D1/D2/D3) as the rest of the season +
  tournament were added; women's went from an unarchived February baseline to
  6,026 / 4,510 / 5,682. Total now = 32,809 games.
* Empty games: MBB dropped from 1,741 empty (D1+D2+D3) to just 47. That is the
  headline win of the re-scrape + empty-shell repair.
* Player records captured: +2,520 across the six accumulated files (49,552 ->
  52,072), because filling the empty games surfaced thousands of player lines
  that were missing before.
* Teams: essentially unchanged (+11), since teams were already listed even in
  empty games; only a handful of new late-season opponents were added.
